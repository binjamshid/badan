# Code of Conduct

Help make Badan a place where people can ask questions, report issues, and contribute in good faith. Treat others with respect regardless of identity, background, ability, experience, or language. Review the work on its merits and explain disagreement in concrete terms.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone else’s private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply in Badan project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner at [support@binjamshid.com](mailto:support@binjamshid.com). Include a short description, the relevant context or project links, and any privacy concerns about handling the report. Share only the information needed to review the incident.

The repository owner reviews reports, limits sharing to people needed to address them, and may remove harmful content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when warranted.
