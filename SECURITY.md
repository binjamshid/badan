# Security and privacy reporting

Email potential vulnerabilities and privacy incidents to [support@binjamshid.com](mailto:support@binjamshid.com) with `Badan security` in the subject. Use this private route for suspected exposure of health information, account or access-control problems, and other security issues. Keep details out of public issues, pull requests, and forks while the owner investigates.

Include enough information to identify and reproduce the issue safely.

- Affected platform, Badan version and build number, or source commit, plus your OS version if relevant.
- The affected feature and the conditions that make the issue possible, including which permissions or optional features were enabled.
- Expected and observed behavior, the potential impact, and steps using an account or device you control and entirely synthetic data.
- A minimal sanitized log excerpt if it adds evidence. Remove identifiers, private URLs, and tokens. Keep real health information, exports, backups, credentials, and raw production logs out of the report and any attachments.

The owner reviews reports for the latest official release on each released platform and for the default branch once it is public. If you observed the issue on an older build or a fork, include the exact version or revision and whether you could reproduce it on the current official build or source.

The owner may ask for clarifying details and coordinate remediation and disclosure with you. If publishing a fix would expose an unremediated vulnerability, coordinate privately with the affected maintainer and follow the timing and notification terms of the limited exception in section 4.
