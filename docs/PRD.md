# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product

BADAN

## Document status

Draft v0.1

## Product summary

BADAN is a privacy-respecting cross-platform health repository and interpretation platform. It collects user-consented health data from mobile health frameworks, devices, integrations, and manual logs; normalizes that data into a canonical model; and renders it in both low-friction casual views and deep power-user workflows.

## Problem statement

Most health apps force an undesirable trade-off. They are either:
- too shallow, closed, and opaque for advanced users, or
- too dense, manual, and intimidating for casual users.

Users also face fragmented data across wearables, phone platforms, nutrition tools, workout apps, medication trackers, symptom notes, and clinical-adjacent records.

BADAN exists to solve fragmentation, opacity, and mismatch of depth.

## Vision

Create the most trustworthy user-controlled health repository in its category: broad in ingestion, clear in explanation, powerful in export, warm in UX, and flexible enough to serve both passive and highly technical users.

## Goals

### Business goals
- Differentiate on exportability, privacy posture, Android parity, and first-class structured logging.
- Build a reusable foundation for advanced analytics and multilingual expansion.

### User goals
- Understand current state quickly.
- Inspect underlying evidence when desired.
- Log symptoms, medications, food, exercise, and issues without friction.
- Export personal data easily.

### Product goals
- One canonical data model serving both casual and power-user experiences.
- Strong ingestion and normalization layer.
- Explainable metrics and insight generation.

## Non-goals
- Diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Regulated medical-device launch posture.
- Single-vendor hardware dependence.

## MVP must include
- ingestion foundation
- canonical data model
- provenance tracking
- home summary
- key metric detail screens
- logging foundations
- CSV/JSON export foundation
- dashboard customization basics
