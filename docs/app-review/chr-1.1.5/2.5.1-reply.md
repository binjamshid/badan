only `HEALTHKIT`. The only real checkbox is Xcode → Signing & Capabilities → HealthKit → Clinical
Health Records, which is already clear because the entitlement key is gone. Removing the key from
`BADAN.entitlements` **is** the whole remediation; the reply says so and offers to do more if Apple
holds a record we cannot see.

Verified against the uploaded build, not just the source: the signed entitlements in `BADAN.ipa`
contain `com.apple.developer.healthkit` and `com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery`
and **no** `com.apple.developer.healthkit.access`; the packaged Info.plist carries
`NSCameraUsageDescription` and no clinical key.

## PASTE THIS

Thank you for the clear instruction. Build 1.1 (6) removes the Clinical Health Records API from
BADAN entirely. The app now contains no HealthKit clinical-records integration of any kind.

Each step you asked for:

- Every reference to HKClinicalRecord and HKClinicalType has been removed from the source. The one
  file that called them was deleted, together with the screen that presented the import and its
  entry point in the app.
- The "health-records" value has been removed from the com.apple.developer.healthkit.access
  entitlement. As your instruction specified, the HealthKit entitlement itself remains enabled, for
  the ordinary read-only HealthKit types BADAN has always used — sleep, heart rate, activity, body
  measurements and similar.
- NSHealthClinicalHealthRecordsShareUsageDescription has been removed from the Info.plist and from
  both the English and Arabic localizations.
- With that entitlement value gone, the app no longer declares the Health Records capability at
  all — it is declared only through com.apple.developer.healthkit.access, and the signed build
  carries com.apple.developer.healthkit and com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery
  only. If any additional step is needed on your side to clear the capability from our record,
  please tell us and we will do it.
- Nothing in the app or its App Store metadata refers to Health Records or clinical records any
  longer. The lab notebook's own copy no longer mentions importing from Apple Health.

The lab notebook feature remains, filled the way it already could be: the user scans a printed
report with the document camera, chooses photos of one, or selects a PDF file. All three are read
by Apple's Vision framework on the device, with no network request, and every recognized field must
be confirmed by the user before anything is saved.

Please let us know if anything further would help.
