BADAN بدن — three directions for a MENA health + AI app

One product, three points of view. Each direction is a complete system — logo, palette, type, and real app screens — built around a different bet on what a body-data app should feel like in the Arab world. Every system is bilingual by construction; one screen per direction is shown in Arabic (RTL).

بدن · “the body” Local-first health repository On-device intelligence Arabic / RTL first-order Wellness-safe · no diagnosis
01  Noor · نور

The calm bet. Warm, editorial, trustworthy — for the passive user who wants reassurance, not a dashboard.

02  Mizan · ميزان

The instrument bet. Dark, precise, signal-dense — for the quantified-self power user who wants the machine.

03  Hilal · هلال

The identity bet. Jewel-toned, geometric, unmistakably regional — modern Gulf confidence, not a translated Western app.

The idea — “first light on the body”

  • نور = light. A dawn arc rising over a solid dot: sunrise over the self. Health as a gentle daily beginning, not an alarm.
  • The dot is the body — the anchor of the whole app (بدن). It never turns red; the arc warms toward gold as your day settles into range.
  • Single-stroke, calligraphic curve reads at 20px as an app icon and engraves cleanly for a splash mark.

DIRECTION 02

Mizan

ميزان

The bet: the power user wants an instrument. Near-black, hairline grids, mono numerals, and a single electric-teal signal — your body as a precise readout.

Logo
BADANquantified · ميزان

The idea — “the scale of balance”

  • ميزان = balance / scale of measure — the classical Arabic instrument of precise judgment. A calibration axis with a balanced beam and a centered node.
  • The node doubles as the dot of ب (baa, the first letter of بدن) — the body sitting at the fulcrum of its own measurements.
  • Monoline, on-grid — engineered to sit in a status bar, a watch complication, or a data-table header without losing itself.

DIRECTION 03

Hilal

هلال

The bet: look unmistakably of the region. Indigo night, a jewel emerald-teal gradient, gold, and a modernised girih star — Gulf-tech confidence, not a translated Western wellness app.

BADANhealth intelligence · هلال

The idea — “the crescent in the eight-fold star”

  • هلال = crescent — the region's oldest symbol of a new cycle, cradled inside a khatam eight-point star (two overlaid squares), the backbone of Islamic geometric art.
  • Jewel gradient = vitality; the crescent is negative space, so the mark stays a crisp silhouette on any surface. Gold spark = the moment of a new insight.
  • Geometry as heritage, gradient as now — signals “built here, built for today” without a single cliché lamp or camel.

Choosing between them

All three honour BADAN's non-negotiables: bilingual/RTL by construction, one calm amber instead of red/green alarm, on-device intelligence, and wellness-safe language. They differ in who they flatter first.

  1. Noor wins if the priority is the passive, reassurance-seeking majority (blueprint's Rania/Omar) and App Store “calm health” shelf appeal.
  2. Mizan wins if the wedge is the quantified-self / power user who'll evangelise the app for its depth and transparency.
  3. Hilal wins if brand distinctiveness in MENA and a premium, regional-tech identity matter most for launch and press.
